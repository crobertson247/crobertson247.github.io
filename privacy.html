Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2023-08-07


PresentPal is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the personal information you provide when using our PresentPal mobile application ("the App").


By accessing or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use the App.


Collection of Information


  1. Information You Provide:

    When you use the App, you may provide personal information such as your name, email address, and other contact details. This information is collected when you create an account, subscribe to premium services, and use features that require personalization.

  2. User Input and Recommendations:

    The App collects and processes the user input provided, such as preferences, interests, and recipient information, to generate gift recommendations. This data is used solely to improve the quality and accuracy of the recommendations.

  3. Premium Subscription and Search History:

    If you subscribe to our premium services, we may collect and store your search history to enhance your experience and provide personalized recommendations based on your search patterns.

  4. Authentication Information:

    The App uses Firebase for user authentication during login and registration. Firebase may collect certain authentication-related information, as described in Firebase's privacy policy.


Use of Information


  1. Personalization and Recommendations:

    We use the information you provide to generate personalized gift recommendations and improve the App's features.

  2. Communication:

    We may use your contact information to send you important notifications, updates, or promotional materials related to the App. You can opt out of these communications at any time.

  3. Analytics:

    We may use aggregated and anonymized data for analytical purposes, such as understanding user behavior and improving the App's functionality.

  4. Service Improvement:

    Your data may be used to enhance the performance, security, and quality of our services.


Data Scaring and Disclosure of Information


  1. Third-Party Services:

    We may share certain information with third-party service providers, such as Firebase for authentication, analytics, and storage purposes. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it solely for the purposes specified by us.

  2. Legal Requirements:

    We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request, including to protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property.


Your Choices


  1. Access and Control:

    You have the right to access, correct, update, or delete your personal information at any time through the App's settings.

  2. Opting Out:

    You can opt out of receiving promotional communications from us by following the instructions provided in the communication.


Security


We take reasonable measures to protect the security of your personal information. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure.



Changes to this policy


We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The updated policy will be posted on our website and within the App.



Contact Us


If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at customer-support@presentpal.org.
